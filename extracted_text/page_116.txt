Several management disciplines relate to the governance of project value and impact. These
clude but are not limited to change management, benefits realization, value management, an
evenue management. Tools and techniques from these disciplines are often incorporated into th
Governance performance domain.
2.1.2 Project Governance Models
Applying the right governance model to a project is an important decision, because too much
governance risks wasting resources, while too little may lead to strategic alignment and project
performance issues. The need to apply optimum governance varies with the implementation approach
and organizational guidelines. The right balance should be made for strategic alignment, compliance,
and delivering value. For these reasons, governance models can be applied at the organizational,
portfolio, program, or project level, depending upon the approach and complexity (see Figure 2-1).
