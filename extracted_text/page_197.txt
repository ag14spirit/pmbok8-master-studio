2.7 Risk Performance Domain
The Risk performance domain represents a comprehensive approach to creating project resilience
by managing risk through risk management practices. The Risk performance domain emphasizes
the project team's ability to anticipate, prepare for, respond to, and adapt to various risks and
disruptions, helping ensure continuity and success under varying uncertainties. The Risk performance
domain advocates for a proactive stance in planning for identified project risks and disruptions,
coupled with adaptive and flexible response mechanisms in case they occur.
