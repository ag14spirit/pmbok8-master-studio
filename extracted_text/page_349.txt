Ethical guidelines in the performing organization, as well as Al policies, could represent a solid basis
for building an organizational culture and establishing a common understanding of how Al should be
used within the organization.
Project professionals should foster a culture of awareness and ethical use of Al, as well as contribute
to increasing responsibility within the team for making ethical decisions related to Al adoption.
