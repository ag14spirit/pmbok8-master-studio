2.1.6.5 Manage Quality Assurance
Manage Quality Assurance is the process of ensuring project processes are performed in a manner
shown in Figure 2-7.
Quality management is sometimes called quality assurance, although quality management has
a broader meaning in non-project work. In contrast, project management differentiates quality
assurance and quality control as two distinct efforts:
• Quality assurance is about using project processes effectively. This effort involves
following and meeting standards to assure stakeholders that the project's final results will
meet their needs, expectations, and requirements. Related concepts include regulations,
compliance, and audits.
• Quality control is about creating project deliverables that meet defined specifications
and thresholds. This effort involves defining the attributes of the value generated by a
project and ensuring project deliverables achieve those attributes. Related concepts include
product design, testing, and defects.
