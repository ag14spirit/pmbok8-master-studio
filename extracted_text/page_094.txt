Moreover, as project changes are encountered, update planning may be required at any appropriate
moment of a project phase. This ongoing refinement of the project management plan is called

The key benefit of this Focus Area is to define the course of action for successfully completing the
project or phase.
When the initial planning effort is complete, the collection of knowledge and artifacts describing the
course of action is often referred to as the project management plan. In some organizations, that
plan is submitted for formal approval and used as the authoritative reference for how the project will
be managed.
Once the project management team is oriented to-and confident with-the course of action, they
are ready to begin executing.?

sensitivity to exceeding an investment-cost threshold.
