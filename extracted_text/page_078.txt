3.8.3 Connected Performance Domains
Teams and stakeholders can affect many aspects of a project, including but not limited to the
following:
• Governance. The project team's proactive and collaborative approach with the governance
eam develops a positive, transparent communication channel with management, helpins
o ensure alignment with the project objectives and achieve outcomes with the fewes
deviations and least amount of confusion.
• Scope. Open-channel communication proactively calibrates to a project's evolving needs
o add elements, adjust quality, or remove elements of the scope or project and quality
requirements.
• Schedule. Empowered teams can offer ideas to accelerate, slow down, or stop delivery of
key project activities to maximize the available opportunities.
Finance. Empowered teams help to reduce or eliminate unplanned expenditures b
erforming steps, requirements, or restrictions that increase the focus on value creation
Benefits realization occurs by generating and identifving long-term goals, so the proiect
delivers the intended identified value.
• Stakeholders. Teams establish, influence, or even define the level and character of
engagement with appropriate stakeholders-both inside and outside the performing
organization.
• Resources. Teams restrict or enable access to physical resources in line with the project
requirements; the same applies for people with the skills, knowledge, and experience
needed to deliver the intended outcomes and promote a learning culture.
• Risk. The team defines the risk thresholds of the project and participates in subsequent risk
management activities.
