• Develop Scope Structure. The process of subdividing project deliverables and project work
into smaller, more manageable components. In agile projects, this effort corresponds to the
decomposition of the product backlog (product backlog breakdown), where work items can
be broken down into epics, features, and user stories.
• Monitor and Control Scope. The process of monitoring the status of the project anc
roduct scope, managing changes to the scope baseline, measuring the quality of the
deliverables, and ensuring fulfillment of the required standards.
• Validate Scope. The process of formalizing acceptance of the completed project
deliverables.
