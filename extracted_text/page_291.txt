Figure 5-13. Precedence Diagramming Method Relationship Types
In the precedence diagramming method, FS is the most commonly used type of precedence
relationship. The SF relationship is very rarely used, but it is included here to present a complete list
of the relationship types in the precedence diagramming method.
Two activities can have two logical relationships at the same time (for example, SS and FF). Multiple
relationships among the same activities are not recommended, so a decision has to be made to
select the relationship with the highest impact. Closed loops are also not recommended in logical
relationships.
