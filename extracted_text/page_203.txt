2.7.2.5 Implement Risk Responses
Implement Risk Responses is the process of implementing sufficient risk response plans. The key
benefit of this process is that it ensures that agreed-upon risk responses are executed as planned to
address overall project risk exposure, minimize individual project threats, and maximize individual
project opportunities (see Figure 2-52).
2.7.2.6 Monitor Risks
Monitor Risks is the process of monitoring the implementation of risk response plans, tracking
identified risks, identifying and analyzing new risks, planning responses for new risks, and evaluating the
effectiveness of risk responses and processes throughout the project. This process helps ensure that risk
owners are assigned to maintain continuity and address emerging risks effectively (see Figure 2-53).
