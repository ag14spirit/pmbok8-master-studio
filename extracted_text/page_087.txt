There is also a meaningful difference between larger-scale methods and larger-scale tool kits. A
method is a systematic way of doing something, typically involving a series of steps or procedures
designed to achieve a specific outcome. As such, methods provide structured approaches to
problem-solving or task completion.
Conversely, a tool kit is a collection of tools, techniques, and resources that can be used to support
various methods or approaches. A tool kit provides flexibility and a range of options to choose from
based on the context and specific needs of a project or task.
PMI Disciplined Agile® (DA°) [3] is designed to be a tool kit.
